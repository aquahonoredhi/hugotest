title: Hi! I’m Mira, an artist, software engineer and open web advocate. 
---

I like **landscape painting, open web protocols, mechanical keyboards** and tinkering with my personal website.

Drawing inspiration from the past, present and future, my work tells _the story of British landscape and the hidden mysteries of time_.

From the ethereal beauty of ancient stones, misty moors and forgotten folklore, to a far flung future, where the natural world has been transformed by technology and climate change, I invite viewers to **embark on a journey through the centuries**.
