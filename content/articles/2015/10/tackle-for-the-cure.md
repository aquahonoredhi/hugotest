Come out and enjoy a night of football for a good cause. Tackle for the Cure will be held on Oct. 9, 2015 at 7:00p.m at Talawanda High School. Tickets can be purchased at the door: \$7 for adults and \$4 for students. Wear pink to support breast cancer awareness month. This is a home game against the Little Miami Panthers.![](/media/tackle-for-the-cure-image1.jpg)

Tackle for the cure is an organization that helps raise money for breast cancer awareness month with proceeds going to the Luna Cares and the Susan G. Komen foundations. Both of these organizations help raise money for cancer research and families that need help paying medical expenses.

Come cheer your Braves on to victory and support a good cause.
