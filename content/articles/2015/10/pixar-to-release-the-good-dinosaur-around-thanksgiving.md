*The Good Dinosaur*, a new 3D animated comedy adventure film from Pixar animation studios, is based on an alternate timeline where dinosaurs never became extinct.

The movie, directed by Peter Sohn, revolves around a young dinosaur, Arlo, whose father was killed in an accident. Arlo accidently falls into a river and is carried far away from his home. He is trying to find his way back and meets a baby human. He befriends the human and together they try to find Arlo’s way home.

The film features the voices of Raymond Ochoa, Jack Bright, Sam Elliott, Anna Paquin, A.J. Buckley, Steve Zahn, Jeffrey Wright, Marcus Scribner, and Frances McDormand, and is scheduled to be released nationwide on November 25, 2015.
