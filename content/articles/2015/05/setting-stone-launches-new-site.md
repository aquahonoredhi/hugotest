![](/media/setting-stone-launches-new-site-image1.png)*Setting Stone Liberal Arts Magazine* has launched a new website to feature student writing and artwork, as well as images from their popular Coffee House event.

While the site is in its infancy, Setting Stone advisor Clare Squance said the group hopes it will bring more attention to the magazine and student work. “Setting Stone has always been about publishing the best, original student work,” she said. “This site just offers another platform for that work to be seen.”

Squance also said Setting Stone still plans to publish a print issue each year, though the new site will publish student writing, art and photography throughout the school year.

Check out the new site [here](http://settingstone.org/).
