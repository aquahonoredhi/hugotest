This year’s SWOC Champions were Kiefer Bell and Hannah Lippincott. Also, Coach Stiver received the boys and girls cross country coach of the year award. “It’s a nice award, but it is truly a reflection of our athletes performing so well,” said Coach Stiver. A total of 31 high school runners got personal records at SWOC, which shows how hard they worked both before and during the race. All of the coaches did an amazing job of preparing the team.

This Saturday on October 24th, 2020 seven male and seven female runners will race in the District Meet at Cedarville University. “We are excited about the District Meet and have every reason to believe we will run well. Our runners look forward to the challenge and the opportunity to qualify to the next level of the tournament,” said Coach Stiver. Based on their performance at SWOC, the team is prepared enough to have a good chance of doing very well at the District Meet. We all hope that our runners may even be able to advance to the next levels such as the Regional and State Meets.
