Next year at Talawanda High School there will be six new classes offered, two classes being split into four, and two classes being combined into one.

The new classes are:

1.  Holocaust Studies (Topical Literature), taught by Mrs. Mann

2.  Adapted Personal Wellness, taught by Mrs. Gregory

3.  Adapted Music, taught by Mr. Marston

4.  Global Popular Music, taught by Mr. Marston

5.  Computer Networking (affiliated with the University of Cincinnati), taught by Mr. Zimmerman

6.  Database Management (affiliated with the University of Cincinnati), taught by Mr. Zimmerman

Students interested in being helpers in Adapted Personal Wellness and Adapted Music should sign up to take the classes on their scheduling sheets. Both marching and concert band classes are splitting into a brass/woodwind and a percussion class for the first time next year. Journalism and Digital Productions are combining into Broadcast Journalism taught by Mrs. Weatherwax and Mr. Zimmerman. Students need to complete an application to take the course which will focus on video and audio journalism and still feature the Daily News. Scheduling papers are due this week.
