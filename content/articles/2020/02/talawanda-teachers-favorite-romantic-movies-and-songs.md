**Valentine’s Day is the perfect opportunity for watching romantic movies. Luckily, there are plenty of them to choose from. Here are some of the favorite romantic movies of Talawanda High School teachers.**

*Dirty Dancing* – Heather Cole

*Love Actually* – Holli Morrish

*My Cousin Vinny* – TJ Pearson

*Return to Me* – Tom York

*Say Anything* – Clare Squance

*Sense and Sensibility* – Kalinde Webb

*So I Married an Ax Murderer* – Adriane Ruther

*Somewhere in Time* – Amy Schulze

*So Who’s Afraid of Virginia Woolf?* – Heidi Schran

*10 Things I Hate About You* – Steve Pasquale

*When Harry Met Sally* – Ted Caudill

Check out this list of the top 33 romantic comedies of all time and compare! <https://www.thrillist.com/entertainment/nation/best-romantic-comedies-of-all-time>

**According to The Knot, some of the most romantic songs are “I Will Always Love You” by Whitney Hueston, “Your Song” by Elton John, “Best of My Love” by The Emotions, and “The Power of Love” by Celine Dion. Talawanda teachers, however, have different ideas:**

1927 – “My Heart Stood Still” – music by Richard Rogers, lyrics by Lorenz Hart, Mr. Pearson

1929 – “Honeysuckle Rose” – Fats Waller, Mr. Pearson

1956 – “I Could Write a Book” – music by Richard Rogers, lyrics by Lorenz Hart, Mr. Pearson

1962 – “Cry To Me” – Solomon Burke, Mrs. Cole

1965 – “My Girl” – Temptations, Mr. York

1966 – “Try a Little Tenderness” – Otis Redding, Ms. Weatherwax

1971 – “Something” – Gorge Harrison, Mrs. Webb

1983 – “Faithfully” – Journey, Mr. Schmid

1986 – “Lady in Red” – Chris de Burgh, Mr. Caudill

1986 – “In Your Eyes” – Peter Gabriel, Mrs. Squance

1992 – “No Scrubs” – TLC, Mr. Schmid

1994 – “Can You Feel the Love Tonight” – Elton John, from the movie *The Lion King*, Mr. Davies

1994 -“I’ll Make Love to You” – Boyz II Men, Mr.Pasquale (Is this school appropriate, Mr. Pasquale?)

1998 – “I Just Don’t Think I’ll Ever Get Over You” – Colin Hay, Mr. Pearson

1998 – “I Don’t Want to Miss a Thing” – Aerosmith, Mr.Pasquale

2014 – “Thinking Out Loud” – Ed Sherran, Mrs. Schulze

2019 – “Meet Me in Memphis” – King Bee & the Stingers, Mr. Pearson
