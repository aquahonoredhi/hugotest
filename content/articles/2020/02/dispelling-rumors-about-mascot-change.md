During Tutorial on Tuesday, Feb. 4, 2020, THS students watched an investigative report by Talawanda’s Channel 85 Productions about the Superintendent Advisory Committee’s suggestion of a mascot change from *The Brave* to *The Titan*s. Following the viewing some rumors began to circulate about the mascot change process. Talawanda Behind the Scenes field reporter Anthony Green interviews Eliot Berberich, a THS reporter and member of the SAC, in order to dispel these rumors. The interview is at:

<https://soundcloud.com/talawandabehindthescenes/talawanda-behind-the-scenes-020620>
