**Talawanda Writing Buddies Talk About Quarantine**

By Susan Slattery’s Stars & Amanda Weatherwax’s Word Wizards

Cover art drawn and photographed by Ella Prytherch, THS Class of 2020

In the 2019-20 school year students in Mrs. Slattery’s fifth grade class at Marshall Elementary and students in Ms. Weatherwax’s ninth grade ELA class at THS were writing partners. They wrote letters to one another and read and responded to one another’s essays and poetry. This spring the students were supposed to meet in person, but they were unable to meet due to the coronavirus pandemic. The teachers thought that it was important to document their final letters to one another in The Talawanda Tribune because they show what our students have been experiencing in these unprecedented times and how they feel about remote learning. We also know that Talawanda’s fifth graders have missed out on their last months of elementary school, and we are so sorry about that. Congratulations to all fifth graders in the Talawanda district. We are so proud of you, and in the words of one of the ninth graders: “You are going to crush it in middle school.”

*Unfortunately due to the nature of remote learning, some students did not log on to respond to their writing partner. That is why there are some letters with no responses. The first letter was written to all of Ms. Slattery’s students because the ninth grader’s writing partner moved away.*

*Dear Fifth Graders:*

*It has been a pleasure to talk to you, and to see all of the work that you have done in class. I’ve loved reading your essays and viewing your wonderful haikus. It truly is a shame that we have had to abruptly end the school year in this way, and can’t do any more projects together, but at least there are some good things to come out of it!*

*One of these things is that there is much more time to do the activities that interest you. I, for example, am cooking far more than I used to cook before this situation involving Covid-19 occurred. Hopefully all of you have found something that you enjoy doing as well.*

*It’s very sad that everybody has to miss the end of school, especially all of you as you are making a very important leap by graduating elementary school. Despite missing the field trips, field day, and other events, it is still an important time as you are finding yourselves entering middle school.*

*I am sure that all of you will love middle school and do great there.*

*Regards,*

*Ninth Grader*
