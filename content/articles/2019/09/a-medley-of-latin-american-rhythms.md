The Musical Program, A Medley of Latin American Rhythms, performed by local and distinguished musicians such as Doug Hamilton, took place in the PAC Friday, Sept. 13 during Sixth Period.

The Musical Program is part of the festivities of the Latin American and Caribbean UniDiversity Festival National Hispanic/Latino Heritage Month Celebration and is sponsored by multiple organizations within our community.

The Latin American and Caribbean Festival

Date: Friday, Sept. 20, 2019

Time: 5:30 pm – 9:00 pm

Location: Oxford Uptown Parks, High St. & Main St., Oxford, OH 45056

?Back up location: Hall Auditorium, Miami University

Sponsored by the Center for American and World Cultures, Global Initiatives with support from Oxford Community Art Center; and Oxford Community Foundation; and in partnership with Alpha Phi Omega; Unidos, Latinx student organization; City of Oxford; Latin American, Latino/a, and Caribbean Studies Program, Department of Global and Intercultural Studies; Department of Spanish and Portuguese; Miami University Center for Student Diversity and Inclusion; Miami University Performing Art Series; Oxford Lane Library; Oxford NAACP Chapter; Oxford Visitors Bureau; Talawanda High school and Talawanda Middle School.
