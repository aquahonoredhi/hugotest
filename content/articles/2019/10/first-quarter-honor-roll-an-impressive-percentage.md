*Tags:* [**Honor Roll**](http://talawandatribune.org/tag/honor-roll/)

The quarterly Honor Roll Breakfast will be held on Thursday, Oct. 31 in the THS Performing Arts Center. Freshmen and sophomores report during 2nd period, and juniors and seniors during 3rd.

A 3.5 GPA is needed for Honor Roll and every grade level has an impressive percentage of students who have qualified: 52% of the freshmen class, 62% of sophomores, 68% of juniors, and 57% of seniors.
