# **Tribune Tries It: Starbucks in the Fall**

*Added by* **Liv Mcgownd** *on October 31, 2023.*![](/media/tribune-tries-it-starbucks-in-the-fall-image1.png)

In honor of the coming of fall, Talawanda Tribune decided to test the fall drinks and foods available at Starbucks. We rated them 1 leaf to 4 leaves based on taste, fall aesthetic, originality, and how much it’s worth standing in line for. Here are the results!

First up, there’s the classic Pumpkin Spice Latte. This drink is an espresso and steamed milk with pumpkin, cinnamon and nutmeg flavors, and is topped with whipped cream. The Tribune members gave this drink 3 leaves for taste, 1 for originality, 3 for fall aesthetic, and a tie between 2 and 3 leaves for how much it’s worth standing in line for.

Some honorable quotes while taste testing include:

“I feel like it’s too much” -Waxy

“It’s very Gilmore Girls”-Liv

Next up, we tasted the Pumpkin Cream Cold Brew. This drink is a Cold brew sweetened with vanilla-flavored syrup, finished with pumpkin cream cold foam and a dusting of pumpkin-spice topping. Our Tribune members overall rated this drink a 4 for taste, 2 for originality, 3 for fall aesthetic, and a 2 for how much it’s worth standing in line for.

Honorable Quotes while taste testing:

“Ew, that’s kinda icky… No it’s good.” -Lily

“That was scrumptious” -Morgan

“Are there negative leaves”-Sadie

For our third drink, we tasted the Iced Apple Crisp Oatmilk Shaken Espresso. This drink is layers of blonde espresso, spiced apple flavors, and creamy oatmilk. This drink was not popular, rated 1 leaf for taste, 4 leaves for originality, 3 for fall aesthetic, and a one for how long it’s worth standing in line for.

Honorable quotes while tasting:

“Smells like a dumpster”- Eliza

“I wanna dump it down the floor vents”-Eliza

“Someone mixed apple cinnamon oatmeal with a dead rat”- Thomas

“The nastiest thing I’ve ever tasted”-Jesse

The last drink we tasted was the Iced Pumpkin Cream Chai Tea Latte. This drink is a blend of black tea infused with warming spices and milk, topped with pumpkin cream cold foam. This drink was well-liked and the majority voted a 4 across all categories.

Quotes while tasting:

“I have so much energy now!!!”-Sadie

The first food item we tasted was the Pumpkin Cream Cheese Muffin. The tribune members rated this a 3 across all categories.

Quotes while tasting:

”Ok ok ok ok delicious” -Jesse

The last food item we tried was the Baked Apple Crossaint. This one is tied between a 3 and a 4 for taste. It received 2 leaves for originality, 2 for fall aesthetic, and 2 for how much it’s worth standing in line for.

Honorable quotes while eating:

“It’s a good croissant… it’s moist” -Garrison

“This one kinda sucks, not gonna lie” -Morgan

Overall, Tribune members’ favorite drink was the Iced Pumpkin Cream Chai tea and their favorite food was the Pumpkin Cream Cheese Muffin. Spice up your fall season by trying this with your friends!
