Welcome to Late To The Party! My name is Will Salzarulo and I love reviewing stuff. Old or new, big or small. However, a lot of the things I wanted to review were made less than 5 years ago. So our advisor, Amanda Weatherwax, gave me a challenge. I never back down from a challenge here are the rules:

1.  The thing being reviewed must be 5 years old or older

2.  Reviews that I write about a newly released thing do not count and will be titled ‘On Time’

3.  All Late to the Party reviews must be marked with “Late to the Party”

Well…

My name is Will Salzarulo and this is Late to the Party.
