Video Games are arguably the most immersive form of entertainment in the world right now. They allow you to transport to new worlds and do things you would have never imagined. Kids these days play games like RDR2, Fortnite, Call of Duty, etc. There are some games, however, that if in existence would take gaming to the next level. Here is a list of the top 10 video game ideas.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image6.png)

Photo Credit: LucasFilm

1\. Star Wars: Rise of the Space Rock

The premise of this game would involve the experience of a typical space rock. In a shocking twist, this space rock lives in the Star Wars universe!!! As the rock in question, you will spend your years drifting through space. To make the game ultra realistic, there would be no controls! To add some spice, you may hear the occasional blaster sound in the background. At the conclusion of the game, you cross paths with another, larger rock and are destroyed.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image1.png)

2\. Five Nights at Kanye’s

Can you survive five nights at the residence of Kanye West? Find out in this hit new game.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image4.png)

Photo Credit: Masterfile

3\. Person Goes to the Movies

We all love going to the movies. Waiting in line, buying tickets, walking to the correct room, and sitting through 20 minutes of previews. Imagine if you could go through that exhilarating experience (and nothing further due to copyright issues) in the comfort of your own home! Look no further than *Person Goes to the Movies*.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image8.png)

Photo Credit: ESRB, Playstation, Hollow Tree Films

4\. Velocipastor: The Video Game

Game based on the cult classic movie *Velocipastor*. Rawr.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image2.png)

Photo Credit: Wikipedia Commons

5\. Talawanda High School Simulator

Wake up, go to school, Can’t go home, no buses.

Photo Credit: Wikipedia Commons

6\. Airplane Simulator

Numba 6. Man Urinates on fellow passenger for not being allowed to smoke!

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image5.jpg)

7\. Big Man Shiv: In Da City

Origins of famous rapper Big Man Shiv before he became famous. We would get Big Man Shiv to be a consultant so we can get the game as accurate as possible

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image9.png)

Photo Credit: BBC

8\. E.T. The Extra-Terrestrial Reboot

A reboot of the classic E.T. on the Atari 2600 made in 1982 with a long development span of 5 weeks. The reboot will follow up with a development time of 5 weeks to invoke the original game.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image3.png)

Photo Credit: NPR

9\. You’re Walking Dead!

This Walking Dead tie-in game with the hit TV Series *Fear The Walking Dead*. You will play as a walker that doesn’t know what to do so you just follow the crowd. The game will have 1 control feature and that’s to make your walker make a noise.

![](/media/the-lemon-s-top-10-video-game-ideas-image7.jpg)

10\. M.I.B. – Minions In Black

A spin-off of Men In Black and Despicable Me. Minions In Black follows Bob as the newest member of the Men In Black and has to defeat an alien lord with help from Gru and the rest of the Despicable Me family.
