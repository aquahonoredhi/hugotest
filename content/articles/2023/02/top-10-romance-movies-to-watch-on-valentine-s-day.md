4.  The Proposal – Hulu

5.  How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days – Prime Video

6.  10 Things I Hate About You – Disney+

7.  Twilight – Hulu

8.  Holidate – Netflix

9.  Mama Mia – Hulu

10. Tangled – Disney+
