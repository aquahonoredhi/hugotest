Oxford, OH — On May 11, 2023, The *Talawanda Tribune* published it’s yearly magazine *Changing Perspectives*. This year’s theme is “styles,” and for the first time in our magazine’s history, human beings are pictured on the front! We also have a fashion spread featuring styles and graphic design by the Tribune’s own Lily Franks and images taken by yearbook photog Hannah Hoblitzell. You can read the magazine [here](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1vTqGsyjYN9_QjJ1ryTS0SXg4wHDzzLve/view?usp=sharing).
