Oxford, OH — Talawanda High School will be one of three schools to host Covid-19 vaccination clinics. Ross High School and Monroe High School will also be hosting.

All people ages 5 and up are now eligible to get the Pfizer Covid-19 vaccination.

Children under the age of 18 need to have a parent/guardian register them for the vaccination and also accompany them to the vaccination site. You must bring an id to the vaccination. You do not need to show proof of citizenship or residency status. Please wear a mask.

Talawanda is not mandating vaccines for staff or students, and as a reminder, individuals need both doses of the Pfizer vaccine to be considered fully vaccinated.

Covid-19 Clinic

By Appointment Only: 10 a.m. to 12 p.m.

Walk-Ins: 12 p.m. to 1 p.m.
