Oxford, OH — Two Talawanda High School seniors, Kiefer Bell and Hannah Lippincott, have qualified for the state meet to be held in Columbus on Sat., Nov. 6, 2021. Hannah will run at 3:00 p.m. and Kiefer will run at 4:00 p.m. Talawanda students, faculty, and marching band gave the runners a big send off during sixth period on Fri., Nov. 5.
