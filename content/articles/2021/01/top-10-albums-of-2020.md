By Hiley Kresse

2020 was a very memorable year, in all regards, but especially musically. To recap the last year, I have compiled this list of my top 10 albums that came out from December 2019 to December of 2020. The list goes as follows:

1.  evermore – Taylor Swift (favorite songs: champagne problems, ivy, and tolerate it)

2.  folklore – Taylor Swift (favorite songs: illicit affairs, betty, and seven)

3.  how i’m feeling now – Charli XCX (favorite songs: 7 years, claws, and detonate)

4.  Future Nostalgia – Dua Lipa (favorite songs: Pretty Please, Physical, and Love Again)

5.  Give Me A Minute – Lizzy McAlpine (favorite songs: Apple Pie, Pancakes for Dinner, and How Do I Tell You?)

6.  Hold It Together (EP) – JP Saxe (favorite songs: 3 Minutes, Explain You, and Hold It Together)

7.  How To Be Human – Chelsea Cutler (favorite songs: Crazier Things, Somebody Else Will Get Your Eyes, and nj)

8.  Ghost World – Lauren Aquilina (favorite songs: Swap Places, Psycho, and Latest Ghost)

9.  Kid Krow – Conan Gray (favorite songs: Wish You Were Sober, Little League, and (Online Love))

10. Fine Line – Harry Styles (favorite songs: Golden, Canyon Moon, and She)
