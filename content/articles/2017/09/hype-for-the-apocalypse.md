“The Walking Dead” is an incredibly popular show on the television channel AMC, and the fanbase is most certainly not extinct here at Talawanda High School.

The television show takes place in a post apocalyptic America, where a County Sheriff, Rick Grimes, is forced to survive in a world ruled by zombies, or, as they call it in the show, “Walkers,” “Roamers,” etc. Several seasons of this show have been created, and coming up on Oct. 22, the eighth season will be added. There is a lot to expect this season.

“I want to see the battle that ensues between the groups,” says Marc Tincher, THS social studies teacher and “Walking Dead” fan.

His prediction for the brand new season is debatable:  
“I think that Negan will win the battle,” Tincher says. Negan is the main antagonist in the series.
