Oxford, OH — That’s right. You heard it here first, ladies and gentlemen! Talawanda High School’s very own “Peanuts” gang has arrived! This thrill seeking musical takes you on many different adventures, from a nail-biting baseball game to a daunting book report about a rabbit that steals food from other people’s gardens. This cast has been working hours on end after school to make the “Peanuts” characters really come to life.

Wesley Howard said, “It is so much fun to be apart of something this big. This is quite the new experience for me and being a part of it really gives me something to love. But you have to remember, it isn’t easy, but can be so much fun when you really devote your time towards something like this and form so many great relationships and friendships with people.” Howard is playing Linus in the THS musical.

The cast have been dancing, singing and perfecting this musical after school so that you can enjoy your time while you watch this comic strip come to life. “Everybody in the cast is so talented and supportive of each other, there is never a dull moment spent with the drama family!” Grace Francis said. Francis will be portraying Lucy.

This “Peanuts” gang is ready for you to enjoy *You’re a Good Man Charlie Brown* from not only a audience perspective, but as a bit of a “Peanuts” character yourself! Show dates are April 19th and 20th at 7:00pm and April 21st at 2:30pm and 7:00pm!
