After singing the National Anthem at Xavier University on Wednesday, Nov. 28 Talawanda High School’s a cappella group Fermata Nowhere did an impromptu performance of Stevie Wonder’s “Superstition” at Steak n’ Shake in Colerain.

Once a restaurant patron realized that the THS students were a part of a singing group, they requested a song. You can view the video of the impromptu performance here:

[Fermata Nowhere Performs “Superstition”](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TtzV5JE0S0Q&scrlybrkr=70c9384b)

Fermata Nowhere is lead by THS choir director TJ Pearson and will be performing in the Winter Choir Concert on Dec. 13 at 7 p.m. in the Talawanda High School Performing Arts Center. The concert will feature all Talawanda High School choirs.
