On Tuesday, Dec. 12, 2016 Talawanda Tribune reporters tried what is all the rage at area elementary schools this holiday season: making gingerbread houses out of graham crackers.

**First, you need to get your supplies which include icing (homemade or store-bought), graham crackers, and candy to decorate your houses:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image14.jpg)

**Next, you decide how you want your house to look. Here one of our teams got some ideas from the internet:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image11.jpg)

**Then you cut your graham crackers. They can break easily so be patient and have plenty of extra just in case:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image12.jpg)

**Then you go to work carefully assembling your graham cracker structure. Here Team 1 makes use of Austin Hibbard’s skills that he learned in Pastry Arts:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image4.jpg)

**Here Team 2 assembles their structure on a foundation of grass made by piping frosting. Mary Kate looks a bit nervous.**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image9.jpg)

**Don’t forget to take a break to enjoy some frosting:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image5.jpg)

**Team 1 also uses their frosting to create some snow on the roof of their structure:**![](/media/tribune-tries-it-gingerbread-wars-image3.jpg)
