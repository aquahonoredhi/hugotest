The Illuminati Card Game has been predicting the future since 1994. It was created by Steve Jackson Games. At first it appears to be just another card game that you play with your friends, but if look through the cards and you’ll see a strange theme: many images on the cards are similar images to various events that happened after the game was printed. Such events include the 9/11 terrorists attacks, the Boston Marathon bombing, and the BP oil spill.

Melody White, a Steve Jackson Gaming warehouse representative, verified that the card game was released in 1994, and that no new cards were produced after the year 1994. She also stated that Jackson’s idea for the card game was developed in the early 80’s.

Two of the game’s cards, titled “Terrorist Nuke” and “Pentagon” show resemblance to the attacks on the World Trade Center in 2001.

![](/media/illuminati-card-game-predicting-the-future-image1.jpg)

This card, titled “Oil Spill,” may relate to the BP oil spill that took place in The Gulf of Mexico.

![](/media/illuminati-card-game-predicting-the-future-image2.png)

The game is available on Steve Jackson’s Gaming website for purchase at a price of \$34.95. Beware of some of the cards that you see–it may be a subliminal message for the future, or maybe, just maybe, it’s a hoax.
