The October Honor Roll breakfast was held on Friday, Oct. 28, 2016. This fall saw 49% of the school’s students making Honor Roll, with a total of 149 ninth graders (55% of freshmen), 125 tenth graders (44% of sophomores), 126 eleventh graders (61% of juniors) and 129 twelfth graders (55% of seniors). Dylan Hacker, senior and Tribune reporter, says that the reason juniors do so well is because the junior teachers are “the best.”
