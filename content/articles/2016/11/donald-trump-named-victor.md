Shortly after the 2016 US Presidential Election, Donald J. Trump gave his victory speech in front of a large excited group of die-hard Trump fans. The fans were filled with happiness and excitement, as they cheer and clap after every single thing that Trump says.

First, Trump starts off with saying that he just received a phone call from runner-up candidate, Secretary Hillary Clinton, conceding to him and congratulating him on his win. Trump congratulates her for running such a great and hard-worked campaign.

Now Trump gets into detail, trying to reach out to all of the people who don’t want him in the President seat. “He says, Now it is time for America to bind the wounds of division, have to get together. To all Republicans and Democrats and independents across this nation, I say it is time for us to come together as one united people. It is time. I pledge to every citizen of our land that I will be President for all of Americans, and this is so important to me. For those who have chosen not to support me in the past, of which there were a few people, I’m reaching out to you for your guidance and your help so that we can work together and unify our great country.”

Trump knows a lot of people dislike him, and he is claiming to be giving out his hand, willing to work with those people who don’t support him. He says he is going to be the President for all Americans, not just for certain people, such as certain races or genders. Trump is known for being in favor of caucasians when it comes to race, and in favor of men when it comes to gender. Many believe he is racist and sexist, but Trump claims to be a fair and equal President to all people of America.

Next, Trump thanks people who helped him through his campaign, which he doesn’t like to call it. He claims his campaign to rather be a movement, “a hard working movement.” He thanks a tremendous amount of people, he goes on thanking people for several minutes, even calling people up to the stage. He thanked people such as his family, the Secret Service, Governor Chris Christie, former Mayor Rudy Giuliani, Senator Jeff Sessions, and plenty more. He thanks all of them and all of his fans for their wonderful support.

Last, he says he will not let anyone down, and that he is going to do a great job. He says he is looking forward to being our President. To finish his victory speech he says, “It’s an amazing evening. It’s been an amazing two-year period, and I love this country. Thank you. Thank you very much.”
