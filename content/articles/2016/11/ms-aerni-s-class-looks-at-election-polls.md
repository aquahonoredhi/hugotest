Throughout the week of Nov. 1st, Ms. Aerni’s American History class discussed the election and tried to find unbiased information in order to figure out which candidate will win. The Tribune got a chance to sit down with a few students to get their opinion on the election. Patrick Geshan,Renowned Braves TV reporter, stated: “I think Hillary will win because she has more voter support in the polls so far.” Sophomore Livi Mullinex told us she’s “very nervous” and glad that she can’t vote. Livi wasn’t the only one thinking this, Sophomore Caleb Boggess told us the same thing. At this point, most of America agrees on this: Tuesday can’t come soon enough.
