On Tuesday, Sep. 8, 2016, at Talawanda High School, your Braves Volleyball team faced the Harrison Wildcats. Going into the night the Wildcats had a record of 4-3 and a conference record of 0-2. The Braves had a record of 5-1 with a conference record of 1-0. The first set of the game was easily won 25-8 by the Braves. The second set wasn’t won as easily for the first but your Braves managed to win the second set 25-20. In the third set the Wildcats tried to rally back from their deficit by putting up a very competitive last match. The Braves battled back and came out with last win of the set, 26-24 and got the win over the Wildcats 3-0.

After the hard- fought win senior player Miklaye Smith talked about her expectations for the rest of the season to come: “Coach always expects high energy, and to leave with a win. So I guess my expectations for this season is to make the season greater than last year!” Since the game Tuesday the Braves went on to play Mount Healthy on Thursday and got the win 3-0.
