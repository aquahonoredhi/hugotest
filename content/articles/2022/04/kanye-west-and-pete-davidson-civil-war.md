Many of you have likely indulged in the musical creations of the popular American rapper, Kanye West (also known as “Ye”), who has released over a hundred songs in his time as a musical artist.

West is well known both for his music and his interesting personality. A perfect example of his personality is his recent beef with popular comedian Pete Davidson.

This beef started when West, with much discontent, divorced from Kim Kardashian, who he married in 2014. Kardashian soon got with Pete Davidson. Not long after, Kanye began publicly revealing his thoughts on the situation through various displays of aggression towards Pete, including an unofficial declaration of war in an instagram post that featured him and other celebrities photoshopped onto the movie poster for “Captain America: Civil War.”

This unofficial civil war has raised a very important question among fans: who would win?

To help you answer that question, I have compiled a list of the participants along with an analysis of their abilities.![](/media/kanye-west-and-pete-davidson-civil-war-image3.jpg)
