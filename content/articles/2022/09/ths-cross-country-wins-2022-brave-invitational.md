Oxford, OH—On Saturday, Aug. 27, 2022, the THS boys and girls took first in the Brave Cross Country Invitational. Max Bixler came in first for the boy’s team with a time of 17:07.10 and Maddox Burns came in second with a time of 17:25.70. Garrison Franks and Bryson Fears finished at fourth and fifth. THS Girls took the top three spots in their race with Lucia Rodbro coming in first at 19:49.80, Adriana Luking in second at 22:33.30, and Rory West Poley coming in third at 23:32.50.

Both Talawanda Middle School teams came in second in their races with an impressive first place finish and middle school course record set by Fletcher Rodbro at 10:58.70 in the boy’s race and stellar first and second place finishes by Katie and Madison Sparks in the girl’s race.
