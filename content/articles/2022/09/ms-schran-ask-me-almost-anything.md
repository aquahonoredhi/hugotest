**Q: Rank the following 5 Beatles songs:**

*Yellow Submarine*

*And I Love Her*

*I Wanna Hold Your Hand*

*Here Comes the Sun*

*Twist and Shout*

A:

*Here Comes the Sun*

*And I Love Her*

*Yellow Submarine*

*I Wanna Hold Your Hand*

*Twist and Shout*

**Q: Biggie or Smalls?**

A: Smalls (but don’t tell Biggie).

**Q: Favorite Scientist?**

A: Richard Feynman.

**Q: Coolest Science fact you know?**

A: You can make molecules stay still with lazers and almost get to absolute zero, which is so cool.

**Q: Where did you play football?**

A: In Cincinnati for the Cincinnati Sizzle and our coach was Ickey Woods.

**Q: Favorite part about football?**

A: Tackling people.

**Q: What would your perfect day consist of?**

A: A bike ride, yoga, an eclipse, hummingbirds, and fresh peaches on the deck.

**Q: Best thing to do in spare time?**

A: Ride my bike.

**Q: Best result of food chemistry (What’s your favorite food)?**

A: Salt.

**Q: Coolest area of science?**

A: Quantum physics.
