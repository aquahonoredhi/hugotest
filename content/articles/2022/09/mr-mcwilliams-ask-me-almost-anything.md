Mr. McWilliams: Ask Me Almost Anything

By MUHAMMED KHAN

Q: Who is better at pranks, you or Mr. Pearson?

**A: Definitely me, because Mr. Pearson is too short to do pranks.**

Q: Why did you cancel “Cookadoodledo?”

**A: Mr. Pearson and I had creative differences.**

Q: What would be your perfect last meal?

**A: Pizza in Italy**

Q: What is the funniest project you have ever assigned or that has been turned in?

**A: Cardboard armor**

Q: What is your opinion about pineapple on pizza?

**A: With ham it is very acceptable, but too much pineapple makes the crust soggy.**

Q: Beef, pork, or veggie hot dogs?

**A: Beef**

Q: Who is your favorite artist?

**A: Alexander Calder**

Q: Who is your least favorite artist? Feel free to name a student.

**A: Andrew Averbach-Rothchild**

Q: Favorite artistic medium?

**A: Metal**

Q: What is your favorite type of cheese?

**A: Tillamook sharp cheddar**
