The whimsical Norwegian talk show duo Bård and Vegard Ylvisåker, also known as *Ylvis*, have pushed their way to the peak of the United States top 100 charts with their most recent song [*The Fox*](http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jofNR_WkoCE), which was released on September 3, 2013.

*The Fox* has had 96 million views on youtube as of October 8, and that number is still on the rise. The song has reached number 8 on the *Hot 100 Billboard*. The song was made as a promotion for the new season on their television show titled *I Kveld Med Ylvis* or *Tonight with Ylvis in english*.

The duo was surprised by the success of the song. “The song was meant to entertain a few norwegians for 3 minutes” said Bård on the *Skavlan Talkshow.*

They have performed the song live at the 2013 *iHeartRadio Music Festival* and on the *Ellen DeGeneres show.* They were recently signed by *Warner Music Group*, one of the “big three” in the recording world.

Ylvis had worked with *Stargate Studios*, big name producers from New York. The Ylvisåker brothers knew who they were working with. They say that their goal wasn’t to make a song that looked like they were trying to make a hit. “from a comedy perspective, it would be much more fun to abuse the talent of *Stargate* and come with a total s\*\*\* idea” said Bård. “But that backfired.”
