Actor, author, and comedian Jim Gaffigan will be performing at Miami University’s Millett Hall on Saturday, October 5 at 8:30 pm as part of his nationwide “White Bread Tour”.

Up and coming comedian Tom Shillue will be opening the show. Shillue has appeared on *Late Night With Jimmy Fallon* and is currently touring with Gaffigan.

Gaffigan has starred in the movies such as *The Love Guru* and *Away We Go,* and has appeared on shows like *Conan* and *The Daily Show*. Gaffigan recently published his first autobiography about his daily life with five children titled *Dad Is Fat*.

Junior Jack Spaulding has been a fan of Gaffigan for almost five years and says he plans on attending the show. “I enjoy stand up comedy because of its impulsive nature of spontaneous jokes off of crowd response,” he said. “Jim Gaffigan is one of the best in the business so I really enjoy watching him and his movies.”

Tickets are still available and are for sale at a set price of \$38. Tickets can be ordered online on Miami University’s box office website [here](http://www.hdg.muohio.edu/BoxOffice/), or by calling 513-529-3200.
