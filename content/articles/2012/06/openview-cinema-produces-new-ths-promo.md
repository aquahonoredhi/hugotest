Openview Cinema (a filmmaking group comprised of former Talawanda students Colton Kidwell, Bryce, Konrad, and Cody Norris) has created a [promotional video](http://youtu.be/lvG2iysgcVI) that highlights the new Talawanda High School (set to open next Fall).

The brief, seven minute video opens with spectacular shots of the new building’s rotunda, tilting skyward to focus on the words “Pride, Integrity, Excellence” as the opening notes of Coldplay’s “Paradise” play. The video features interviews with high school staff, as well as district administrators who discuss exciting new aspects of the new building. Footage in the video shows impressive elements of the new building, such as athletic facilities, science labs, classrooms, and the new, state-of-the-art auditorium.

To view the film, visit [youtube](http://youtu.be/lvG2iysgcVI).
