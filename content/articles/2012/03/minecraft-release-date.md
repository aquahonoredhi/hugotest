If you enjoyed mining some pixels, exploring some dungeons, and fighting the creeper on the PC, then you’ll be excited to know that popular indie game *[Minecraft](http://www.minecraft.net/)* will be coming to the Xbox 360 on May 9th.

Now, some of you might be asking… “Justin, how would *Minecraf*t work on the Xbox?” Well, my friends, as with all things, there will be some changes to the game. Parts of which include a new crafting system, Kinect support (but not on release), and even the ability to play split-screen… online and offline.

Due to the game being under a contract with Microsoft, it will not be released on Sony’s PlayStation 3.

The price is 1600 Microsoft Points (or \$20) on its May 9th release.
