The Presidential Elections are drawing near; soon, the candidates will have to choose their campaign slogans.

Often times these slogans are based on something the candidates have said. Here are some of the slogans in the works for the 2012 bid for the Whitehouse.

**Mitt Romney**

*Quote*: “I like being able to fire people who provide services to me.”

*Possible Slogan*: “Mitt Romney, Firing America, 2012” or “Romney, the TEMPORARY Job Creator”

*Quote*: “I believe in an America where millions of Americans believe in an America, that’s the America millions of Americans believe in. That’s the America I love.”

*Possible Slogans*: “Mitt Romney, Leading America Into Confusion, 2012” or “America, Romney the American is the American for the Job, America”

**Newt Gingrich**

*Quote*: “If the Soviet Empire still existed, I’d be terrified. The fact is we can afford a fairly ignorant president now.”

*Possible Slogans*: “Lower your expectations, America. Gingrich 2012” or “Gingrich, the Most Ignorant Man for the Job” or “Gingrich; Red, White, Blue and Ignorant”

*Quote*: “Give the park police more ammo.” – In response to a reporter question regarding the homelessness problem, a few days after police shot a homeless person near the Whitehouse.

*Possible Slogan*: “A Vote for Gingrich is a Vote for Hobo Genocide”

**Rick Santorum**

*Quote*: “Higher income people don’t have pay taxes if they don’t want to”

*Possible Slogan*: “Rick Santorum, He’s Got Romney’s Vote”

**Ron Paul**

*Quote*: “1913 wasn’t a very good year.”

*Possible Slogan*: “Ron Paul, the Perfect Candidate for 1913”

**Barrack Obama**

*Quote*: “The Middle East is obviously an issue that has plagued that region for centuries”

*Possible Slogan*: “Obama, Saving the Middle East From Itself”
