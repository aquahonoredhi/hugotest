When I was little I watched nothing but Dora. I felt a special connection with Dora, as if we were sisters. Growing up I didn’t have many friends and my mom was always busy. Dora was the only one there to teach me lessons, and boy did she teach me.

**Never let enemies stop you.** Swiper was constantly getting in Dora’s way and trying to steal her stuff. Dora never let that stop her from getting where she needed to be.**There is always a way.** Dora would come across obstacles everyday. She wanted to get there so bad that she would always find a way**It’s okay to ask for help.** Dora would have some difficulties with almost everything she did. She would always find a way to fix it, but it wasn’t on her own. She wasn’t afraid to ask us for help, and she didn’t even know us.**Your family will always be there for you.** Dora always had help from her family. Her cousin Diego would help sometimes. She also had a pet monkey that helped her. This also kind of showed me that family doesn’t have to be blood, just someone that cares. **It is good to help everyone.** Dora always helped anyone in need. One time she even helped a bad guy. He was being really mean but he seemed upset so she asked him what was wrong. He told her and then she helped him. Everyone has problems so it’s kind to help.
